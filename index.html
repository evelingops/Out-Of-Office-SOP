✉️  3. Also Set Gmail Vacation Responder for Any Day Off

The Calendar OOO handles Chat status automatically, but you must also set Gmail's vacation responder so anyone who emails you gets an automatic reply.

1

Open Gmail → click ⚙️ gear icon → "See all settings"

The gear icon is in the top-right of Gmail.

2

"General" tab → scroll to "Vacation responder" → toggle ON

🚨  Lead & Task Assignment Policy — No Exceptions
🚫
Gray icon, lunch status, or OOO = skipped. If your Chat status shows anything other than Active, you will not receive leads or tasks. No exceptions.
📅
Calendar OOO is mandatory for full-day absences — PTO, doctor visits, flights, any day you are not working. Set it in advance, not the morning of.
⏱️
Timed status is required for short breaks. Stepping away for lunch or an errand? Set a timed status in Chat (e.g. "🍱 At Lunch" for 1 hour). Do not just go silent.
🔁
Reset everything when you return. Turn off your Gmail vacation responder and confirm your Calendar OOO event has ended so leads flow back to you normally.
💬
Notify Eveling before any day off and submit the Out of Office form. Do not rely solely on your status — direct communication is required.
