Team Status & Out of Office Guide

Cactus Home Offer — Required procedures for all team members

⚠️  All team members must follow these steps when going to lunch, taking a day off, or going on vacation. Sales team members will not receive leads while marked as Out of Office.
🍽️ 1. How to Set Your "On Lunch" Status in Google Chat
1

Click your profile photo in the top-right corner of Google Chat

Look for your initials or picture in the upper-right of Google Chat or Gmail. Click it to open your account menu.

2

Find the status options — click your current status or "Set a status"

A panel opens showing your name, email, and status choices.

3

Select "On lunch" — your status dot turns yellow 🟡

Your avatar now shows a yellow dot. Anyone who tries to message you in Chat will see your lunch status before sending.

4

When you return — set yourself back to "Active" ✅

Repeat the same steps and select Active so your team knows you are back and available.

💡 Tip: You can set a custom duration (e.g. "Back in 1 hour") so the status clears automatically.
🌴 2. How to Set Out of Office (OOO) in Gmail for Vacation / Days Off
1

Open Gmail → click the ⚙️ gear icon → "See all settings"

The gear icon is in the top-right of Gmail. Click it, then click "See all settings" in the panel that slides in.

2

Go to the "General" tab → scroll down to "Vacation responder"

Stay on the General tab (it is the default). Scroll to the bottom to find the Vacation responder section.

3

Turn it ON and fill in your dates, subject, and message

Toggle Vacation responder ON. Fill in: First day, Last day, Subject (e.g. "Out of Office – Back June 15"), and a message with your return date and who to contact for urgent matters.

4

Also set your Google Chat status to Away / Out of Office 🌴

Repeat Section 1 steps — click your avatar in Chat and add a custom status like "On vacation – back June 15." Teammates see it before they message you.

🚨 Sales Team — Lead Policy During Vacation / OOO
🚫
No leads will be passed to a sales team member while they are marked as Out of Office. The lead manager will hold all leads until you return.
📅
Your OOO status is your responsibility. If you forget to set it, leads may be lost or misrouted. Always set your status before you leave.
🔁
When you return, immediately set your status back to Active in Chat and disable the Gmail vacation responder so leads flow back to you normally.
💬
Notify your team lead before your day off so coverage can be arranged. Do not rely solely on the OOO status.
